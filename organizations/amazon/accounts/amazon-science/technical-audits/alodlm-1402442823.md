## Project purpose

Diffusion language models (DLMs) enable fast generation by predicting multiple tokens in parallel, yet their practical adoption remains hindered by a persistent quality gap relative to comparably sized autoregressive models. We attribute this gap to a *computation-difficulty mismatch*: within a partially observed sequence, some unknown tokens are readily predictable, while others require substantially more computation to resolve. Existing DLMs, however, apply uniform computational depth to every unknown position at each denoising step.
