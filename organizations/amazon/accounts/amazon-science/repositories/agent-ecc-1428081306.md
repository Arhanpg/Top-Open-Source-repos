### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> **Version-consistent experience consumption for agent training.** Validate recorded experience against a chosen verifier version, re-evaluate it when needed, and track what each training step actually consumes. Agent ECC is the open-source implementation of *Epoch-Consistent Commit*. In asynchronous agent training, trajectories are generated, graded and buffered long before a learner step consumes them, while the things that decide their labels keep changing: test labels are fixed, test suites are hardened, evaluators are patched, worlds are refreshed.
