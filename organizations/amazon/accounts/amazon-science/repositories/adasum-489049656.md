> This repository contains the main codebase for the corresponding NAACL findings paper. In this work, we explored in-domain information storage to adapters by pre-training them on customer reviews via the leave-one-out objective. Further, we fine-tune the pre-trained adapters on a handful of summaries. This method yields state-of-the-art results in terms of ROUGE scores and reduces semantic mistakes in generated summaries.
