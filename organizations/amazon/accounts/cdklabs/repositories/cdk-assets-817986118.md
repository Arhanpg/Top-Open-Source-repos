### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> A tool for publishing CDK assets to AWS environments. `cdk-assets` requires an asset manifest file called `assets.json`, in a CDK CloudAssembly (`cdk.out/assets.json`). It will take the assets listed in the manifest, prepare them as required and upload them to the locations indicated in the manifest. Currently the following asset types are supported: - Files and archives, uploaded to S3 - Docker Images, uploaded to ECR - Files, archives, and Docker images built by external utilities S3 buckets and ECR repositories to upload to are expected to exist already.
