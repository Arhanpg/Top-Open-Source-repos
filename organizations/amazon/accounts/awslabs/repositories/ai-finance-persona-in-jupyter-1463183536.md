### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> **FinTyche** is a Jupyter AI persona for quantitative finance. It follows the same architecture as `jupyter-ai-quickagent` — a persona registered through the `jupyter_ai.personas` entry point and managed by `jupyter-ai-persona-manager` — except FinTyche is a single, fixed persona rather than an agent builder. Agentic AI offers a third leg of a stool that comprises (i) financial packages, (ii) Jupyter notebooks, (iii) agentic AI. Many financial libraries exist that are used in Jupyter notebooks already. Some popular ones are listed below. This repository builds a natural language workflow Persona into Jupyter AI, that enables users to price securities and undertake financial analyses
