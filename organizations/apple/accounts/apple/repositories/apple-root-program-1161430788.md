### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> This repository contains the official policy documentation and resources for the Apple Root Program. The Apple Root Program establishes requirements for Certification Authorities (CAs) whose root certificates are included in Apple operating systems and devices. This includes requirements for certificate issuance, security practices, and operational standards. - Apple Root Certificate Program Policy - The complete policy governing participation in the Apple Root Program The policy was previously available at apple.com/certificateauthority/ca_program.html. For questions about the Apple Root Program, please contact certificate-authority-program@apple.com. Copyright © 2026 Apple Inc. All rights reserved.
