### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> FHIRModels ========== FHIRModels is a Swift library for [FHIR®][fhir] resource data models. - Native Swift representation of FHIR resources, elements and data types - Separate targets for DSTU2, STU3, R4, R4B, R5 and latest build versions - Enforced non-nullability of mandatory parameters - Enums for most closed code systems - Enums to support value[x] types - Date/Time parsing, validation and conversion to and from NSDate - Swift `Codable` and `Sendable` FHIRModels works with Swift 5.7 and newer. 1. In Xcode 11 and newer, go to “File” » “Add Package Dependencies...” 2. Search for “FHIRModels” in the panel that comes up
