## Project purpose

QComputeSDK-TypeScript is the TypeScript version of QCompute, the quantum development kit of Quantum Leaf. QComputeSDK-TypeScript can be used to design quantum experiments: build quantum circuits, manipulate quantum modules, and run quantum computing tasks on Quantum Leaf ( ). The user, via QComputeSDK-TypeScript, can use all quantum-ends on the cloud, including QPUs and cloud simulators.
