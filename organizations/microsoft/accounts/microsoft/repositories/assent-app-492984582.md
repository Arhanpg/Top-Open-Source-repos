## Upstream description

Microsoft Assent is an enterprise scale product, internally used at Microsoft. It delivers a modern approvals experience for any approval, anywhere, anytime, on any device. It consolidates approvals across organization's line of business applications, building on modern technology and powered by Microsoft Azure. It delivers a unified approvals experience for any approval on multiple form factors (i.e., Website, Outlook Actionable email, Teams), anytime.
