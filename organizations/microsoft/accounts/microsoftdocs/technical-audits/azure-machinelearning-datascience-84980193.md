## Project purpose

This web service performs market basket analysis to produce product recommendations of products that of frequently bought together from historical transactions provided by the user. Frequently Bought Together recommendations helps shoppers identify products in a catalog that are most relevant when purchasing a specific item. Prominently showing these recommendations have proven effective in lifting sales for online retailers.
