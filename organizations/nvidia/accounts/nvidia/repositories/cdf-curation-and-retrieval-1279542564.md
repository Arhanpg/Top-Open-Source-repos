## Upstream description

PAIDF Curation and Retrieval turns raw video and image collections into training-ready datasets. It runs NVIDIA Cosmos Curator for splitting, filtering, captioning, embedding, deduplication, and sharding, and NVIDIA TAO Data Services for data mining, nearest-neighbor retrieval, and distribution matching.
