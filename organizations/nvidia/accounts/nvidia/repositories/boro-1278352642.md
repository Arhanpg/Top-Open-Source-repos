### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> Boro is an AI-assisted kernel development CLI for developers working inside their own git tree. It is meant for the stages before a patch series is posted publicly, or for downstream work that may never follow the normal mailing-list review path: backports, distro kernel maintenance, security-fix integration, and other local patch-stack iteration.
