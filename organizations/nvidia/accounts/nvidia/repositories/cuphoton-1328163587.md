## Upstream description

CUDA-accelerated Python tools for astronomical imaging, radio interferometry, and X-ray trace analysis. Align and subtract FITS images, reconstruct scenes from multiple exposures, fit and score transient candidates, correlate time series, and monitor results in live dashboards.
