### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> This repository holds NVIDIA-maintained utilities to improve GPU performance and usability for Tensorflow training and inference. The intent of Atex is to make up-to-date utilities available to users as quickly as possible. As such, these utilities are experimental. Some we will upstream and support permanently in projects such as TensorFlow or Keras.
