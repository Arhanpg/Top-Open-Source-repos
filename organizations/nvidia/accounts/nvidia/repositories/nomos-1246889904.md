## Upstream description

Stops AI agents from taking unwanted or overeager actions. Nomos reviews each proposed action against the agent's own context (its permissions, task, justification, blast radius and signed receipts of what it has observed) using OPA policy and an LLM council. Approved actions get a signed, single-use Writ; the executor runs exactly that.
