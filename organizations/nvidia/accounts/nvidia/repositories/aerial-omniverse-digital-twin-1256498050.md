### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> NVIDIA Aerial™ Omniverse Digital Twin (AODT) is a collection of frameworks and software tools for next-generation digital twins, enabling cutting-edge research and development of 5G and 6G wireless systems. AODT enables physically accurate simulations of complete 5G and 6G systems, from a single tower to city scale. It incorporates software-defined radio access network (RAN) and user-equipment simulators, along with realistic terrain and object properties. Researchers can use AODT to simulate and build base-station algorithms from site-specific data and train models in real time to improve transmission efficiency.
