### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> **Automated failure analysis for models and agent harnesses.** A reward tells you *that* an agent failed, not *why*. AutoFA is a toolkit for reading completed evaluation runs (local **NeMo Gym** and **Harbor** folders or remote **EvalRDL** runs) and explaining their failures from complementary angles: bottom-up discovery and top-down taxonomy classification run in parallel on the same data.
