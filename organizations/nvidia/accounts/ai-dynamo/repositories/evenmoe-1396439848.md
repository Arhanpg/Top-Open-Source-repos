### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> A framework-independent library for expert placement and token routing in Mixture-of-Experts inference aiming to solve the MoE load imbalance issue and improve the MoE model serving efficiency. EvenMoE brings placement and routing policies behind a unified Python API, with adapters for different inference backend. EvenMoE balances work at two levels: - **L1 — Expert placement:** chooses which logical experts occupy physical expert slots across GPUs, including redundant replicas. - **L2 — Token routing:** selects a physical replica for each logical routing expert selected by TopK and, when enabled, a destination GPU for shared-expert computation.
