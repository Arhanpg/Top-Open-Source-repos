### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> When users hear Knowledge Graph (KG), or graph in general, there is the assumption that we are talking about a Graph Database. While a graph database is one way to implement a knowledge graph, we are not building a graph database. Therefore, we are not focusing on ACID compliance, being transactional, or worrying about multiple users. Our focus is just on being able to capture a KG. The KG will also function as an DGL or PyG GraphStore. The focus of this design are as follows: * Homogeneous Graphs for GNN Training and general Graph * Heterogeneous Graphs for GNNs
