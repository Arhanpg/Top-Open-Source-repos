### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> **From Multi-Agent to Single-Agent: When Is Skill Distillation Beneficial?** Multi-agent systems (MAS) decompose tasks into specialized sub-agents, but incur coordination overhead, context fragmentation, and brittle phase ordering. Distilling a MAS into a single-agent skill can bypass these costs — but when does it actually help? We introduce **Metric Freedom** (*F*), the first *a priori*, metric-level predictor of skill utility. *F* measures how tightly output variation couples with score variation (via a Mantel test). Guided by *F*, we propose a **two-stage adaptive distillation framework**: - **Stage 1 (Adaptive Extraction)**: Selectively distills tools and knowledge; discards restrictive pipeline structures on "free" metrics
