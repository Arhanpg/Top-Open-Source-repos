## Attribution and scope

- These ten accounts are designated `official-directory-listed` because SAP's verified organization profile names them as dedicated organizations for SAP projects and resources.
- A listing in SAP's profile establishes the relationship SAP publicly documents; it does not mean every repository in an account was authored by SAP employees or is legally owned by SAP.
- Every account remains independently inventoried by its actual GitHub login.
- The account list is the set explicitly named in the profile section inspected here. New accounts may be added if SAP publishes additional official links.

## Inventory completion

The collector must complete public-repository pagination for SAP and each of the ten additional accounts. Account discovery, inventory completion, README capture and manual technical review remain separate status dimensions.
