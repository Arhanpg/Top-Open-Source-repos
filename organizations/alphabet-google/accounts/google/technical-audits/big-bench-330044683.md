## Project purpose

The Beyond the Imitation Game Benchmark (BIG-bench) is a *collaborative* benchmark intended to probe large language models and extrapolate their future capabilities. The *more than 200* tasks included in BIG-bench are summarized by keyword here, and by task name here. A paper introducing the benchmark, including evaluation results on large language models, is currently under review, and is available as a preprint.
