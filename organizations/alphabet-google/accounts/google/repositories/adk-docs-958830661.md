> **An open-source, code-first toolkit for building, evaluating, and deploying sophisticated AI agents with flexibility and control.** Agent Development Kit (ADK) is a flexible and modular framework for **developing and deploying AI agents**. While optimized for Gemini and the Google ecosystem, ADK is **model-agnostic**, **deployment-agnostic**, and is built for **compatibility with other frameworks**.
