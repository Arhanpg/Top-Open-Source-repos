### Short excerpt (up to 100 words)

> Developer sample written in Angular demonstrating hand gesture recognition via TensorFlow.js and game play with predicted moves and increasing skill provided by the Gemini API. This project was generated with Angular CLI version 17.0.5 and uses tensorflow.js, a library for machine learning in Javascript. Video from the webcam is passed to a pre1.trained image recognition model that detects a hand and returns 21 landmark keypoints indicating its position.
